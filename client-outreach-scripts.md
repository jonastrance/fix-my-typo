# Client Outreach Scripts

## Script 1: Initial Contact (Cold Outreach)

- "Professional Proofreading for Your [Blog/Website/Business]"
- "Quick Question About Your Content Quality"
- "Free Typo Check for [Company Name]"

```

I came across your [blog/website/company] and really enjoyed reading about [specific topic/article]. Your content is engaging and informative!

I noticed a few small typos in [specific page/article] that could be quickly fixed to make your content even more professional. As a proofreading specialist, I'd be happy to review the page for free and send you a detailed list of corrections.

Would this be helpful to you? No strings attached - I just love helping businesses put their best foot forward with error-free content.

Looking forward to hearing from you!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Tips for Using These Scripts

1. **Personalize Every Message**: Replace [Name], [Company], and specific details
2. **Research First**: Mention something specific about their business/content
3. **Keep It Brief**: Busy people appreciate concise messages
4. **Show Value**: Focus on benefits, not just features
5. **Include Call-to-Action**: Make it clear what you want them to do next
6. **Professional Tone**: Proofread your own messages carefully!
7. **Follow Up**: Don't be afraid to send 1-2 follow-ups if you don't hear back
8. **Track Results**: Note which scripts work best and refine them

## Timing Recommendations

- **Initial Outreach**: Monday-Wednesday, 9 AM - 11 AM (recipient's timezone)
- **Follow-Up**: Wait 3-5 business days before following up
- **Thank You Messages**: Within 24 hours of completing the work
- **Upsell Messages**: 2-3 weeks after last project completion
